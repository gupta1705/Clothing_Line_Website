More about shopping at Pradhan by Panchayat

Every village has its pradhan: the one people trust, who stands up for the community. We took the name because that is the role we want to play for India’s village artisans. We carry their craft to people who will value it, and bring a fair living back home.

Pradhan by Panchayat is the clothing line from TVF’s Panchayat: kurtas, Nehru jackets, half sweaters, shirts and pajamas inspired by Phulera, and made by hand by weavers, knitters and tailors in villages across India. Every thread has a village.
