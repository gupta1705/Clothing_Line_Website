2020

Phulera arrives on screen

TVF’s Panchayat introduced India to Phulera: a sleepy village office, a water tank, and people who look after each other.

The wardrobe

Viewers kept asking

Pradhan ji’s half sweater. Sachiv ji’s checks. Prahlad Cha’s gamchha. Phulera’s clothes became as loved as its people.

2026

Pradhan by Panchayat

We took the name because that is the role we want to play for India’s village artisans. Weavers in Chanderi, block printers in Kutch, women stitching Kantha in Bengal: their skills have been passed down for generations, yet too few people ever get to wear what they make.

Not only skilled,
but proud hands behind.

Pradhan by Panchayat brings their work to a wider market, in clothes designed for comfort, dignity and everyday wear. We work directly with artisan families, pay fairly and on time, and make in small batches. Every piece carries a village in its threads.

Gifting for a wedding or your office this Diwali? Tell us what you need and the Panchayat will sort it out.
